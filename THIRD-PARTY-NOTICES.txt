----------------
Packaged-food data is provided by Open Food Facts:
https://world.openfoodfacts.org

The Open Food Facts database is available under the Open Database License
(ODbL) 1.0. Attribution and share-alike terms apply:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

the package label and keeps user corrections local.
