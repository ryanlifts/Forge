iOS App Privacy Policy

Effective August 9, 2026

BlackPyre for iOS has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, or BlackPyre server. Your settings, nutrition and training logs, saved foods, programs, weights, and recovery data stay on your iPhone unless you deliberately export, copy, or share them.

Information stored on your iPhone

BlackPyre stores the information you enter in local app storage. The native iOS app also maintains device-only recovery files and can create backup files in Files → On My iPhone → BlackPyre. Those app-owned files are excluded from automatic iOS/iCloud device backup. Deleting the app removes its local storage, Native Vault, BlackPyre folder, and backups kept only there. A copy you explicitly saved elsewhere remains wherever you placed it.

Apple Health data

Apple Health access is optional. If you choose to connect it, BlackPyre requests separate access to read body weight, active energy, steps, sleep duration, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, workouts, and workout heart rate. BlackPyre may also request permission to write completed workouts that have a real recorded duration. You can allow or refuse each Apple Health data type.

Imported Health data stays on this iPhone. BlackPyre stores only daily totals or latest daily values and per-workout heart-rate averages and maxima. It does not store raw heart-rate samples, routes, or step timelines. The Health cache is separate from BlackPyre logs, excluded from iOS/iCloud device backup, never included in BlackPyre backup or recovery files, never used for advertising, and never sent to BlackPyre or another server. If the cache is lost, BlackPyre can read the source again from Apple Health.

Active energy is displayed separately from BlackPyre's estimate based on food and weight logs and never changes that estimate or your targets. To change or revoke access, open iOS Settings or the Health app, then review BlackPyre under Health access and devices. If an expected signal is missing, check whether the original watch, scale, or app is writing it to Apple Health.

Online food searches

When you search for a packaged food or look up an unknown barcode, BlackPyre sends the search words or barcode to Open Food Facts over HTTPS. Open Food Facts receives the request and associated network information such as your IP address. BlackPyre does not send your food log, name, targets, weight, or training history with that request. See the Open Food Facts privacy information.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to scan food barcodes and, if you choose, to take a meal photo for an AI handoff you send yourself. Barcode recognition runs inside the app. BlackPyre does not upload camera images. A photo selected or taken for an optional AI handoff remains in memory until you share, clear, or finish the handoff; BlackPyre does not transmit it to an AI service.

AI copy/paste handoffs

BlackPyre never contacts an AI provider. It can prepare text or a selected photo for you to copy or share. Any transfer occurs only when you choose another app or service, and that service's privacy terms then apply.

External links and notifications

Exercise-video search opens YouTube with the exercise name in the search query. Research and legal links open the selected external website. Local notifications are used for rest timers only and are managed by iOS.

Backups, export, and deletion

Backup, training-plan, report, recovery, and diagnostic files are created only when you request them. They can contain sensitive personal information, so store them securely. Imported Apple Health data is never included in those files. To delete local information, open Settings → Data & recovery → Erase all BlackPyre data and confirm twice. Protected mode blocks destructive reset until recovery is resolved. Uninstalling the app also removes local data, including the device-only Health cache, but not copies you previously saved elsewhere.

Children

BlackPyre is designed for ages 13 and older. The calculator is not designed for children under 13.

Changes and contact
