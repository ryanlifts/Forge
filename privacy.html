Web App Privacy Policy

Effective August 9, 2026

The BlackPyre web app has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, or BlackPyre server. Your settings, nutrition and training logs, saved foods, programs, weights, and recovery data stay in this browser or installed web app unless you deliberately export, copy, or share them.

Information stored on your device

BlackPyre stores the information you enter in local app storage. In the web app, live data and recovery snapshots remain in this browser or installed web app's site storage and can be lost if that storage or app is removed. Downloaded backup files are separate, but you should keep another copy somewhere you control.

Online food searches

When you search for a food or barcode, BlackPyre first downloads only the matching section of the versioned BlackPyre Food Catalog from GitHub over HTTPS. GitHub receives the request and ordinary network information such as your IP address. The catalog files contain source-identified USDA food records and no BlackPyre user information. If the catalog has no match, BlackPyre may send only the search words or barcode to Open Food Facts over HTTPS as a separately labeled fallback; Open Food Facts also receives ordinary network information such as your IP address. BlackPyre never sends your food log, name, targets, weight, training history, or saved corrections with either request. See GitHub’s privacy statement and the Open Food Facts privacy information.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to scan food barcodes and, if you choose, to take a meal photo for an AI handoff you send yourself. Barcode recognition runs inside the web app. BlackPyre does not upload camera images. A photo selected or taken for an optional AI handoff remains in memory until you share, clear, or finish the handoff; BlackPyre does not transmit it to an AI service.

AI copy/paste handoffs

BlackPyre never contacts an AI provider. It can prepare text or a selected photo for you to copy or share. Any transfer occurs only when you choose another app or service, and that service's privacy terms then apply.

External links and notifications

Exercise-video search opens YouTube with the exercise name in the search query. Research and legal links open the selected external website. Web notifications, where supported and enabled, are used for rest timers only and are managed by your browser or device.

Backups, export, and deletion

Backup, training-plan, report, recovery, and diagnostic files are created only when you request them. They can contain sensitive personal information, so store them securely. To delete local information, open Settings → Data & recovery → Erase all BlackPyre data and confirm twice. Protected mode blocks destructive reset until recovery is resolved. Removing the installed web app or clearing its site data also removes local data, but not backup files you previously saved elsewhere.

Children

BlackPyre is designed for ages 13 and older. The calculator is not designed for children under 13.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when BlackPyre's web data practices change. Developer: Ryan Allen Wilsey. For a private privacy or support question, email blackpyrestrong@gmail.com. You may also use BlackPyre Support for questions that contain no personal information. Do not post personal health, nutrition, or training information publicly.
