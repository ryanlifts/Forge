Support

blackpyrestrong@gmail.com

For a question containing no personal information, you may open a public support request.
Create a BlackPyre support request
Do not include personal health, nutrition, training, backup, or diagnostic data in a public request.

Before requesting help

  • Open Settings → Help & FAQ for instructions covering food, training, backups, and recovery.
  • If saving is paused, do not uninstall. Follow the Protected mode recovery screen.
  • For missing packaged food, verify the package label and use manual entry.
  • Keep a backup copy outside the BlackPyre folder before deleting or replacing the app.

Privacy and deletion

Read the BlackPyre Privacy Policy. Local data can be removed from Settings → Data & recovery → Erase all BlackPyre data. Copies you exported elsewhere must be deleted from those locations separately.
